What STUCK.CENTER is—and isn't

STUCK.CENTER is an independent information and user-interface research project from Useless Instruments. It does not provide emergency response, therapy, diagnoses, legal advice, or individually tailored financial advice. Calculators, when released, will be educational estimates, not decisions or guarantees. Local laws and personal safety circumstances vary.

This page uses no analytics, cookies, third-party fonts, external scripts, or contact forms. Budget inputs are never saved or transmitted by this page. Network providers and devices may still record visits; external links have their own privacy policies. Private browsing does not guarantee safety.
