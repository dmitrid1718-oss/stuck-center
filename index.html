Sometimes staying is costly. Sometimes leaving is costly. Sometimes neither choice is safe or simple. This space is for understanding the practical realities—not for telling anyone what to do.

Privacy and safety: Websites and browser history may be monitored. The “Leave this page” link only navigates away; it does not erase history, notifications, or monitoring records. If someone may monitor your device, consider using a safer device to seek help.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Evidence before conclusions

Research findings, survey claims, policy changes, and first-person experiences need different treatment. We will not turn limited surveys into estimates for every family, or present relationship advice as universally safe.

What STUCK.CENTER is—and isn't

STUCK.CENTER is an independent information and user-interface research project from Useless Instruments. It does not provide emergency response, therapy, diagnoses, legal advice, or individually tailored financial advice. Calculators, when released, will be educational estimates, not decisions or guarantees. Local laws and personal safety circumstances vary.

No user accounts, questionnaires, or personal-data collection are part of this first page. External resources have their own privacy policies. Never rely on a quick-exit link to hide browsing activity.
